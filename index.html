The Artist

A trained musician and a working actor.

Multi-faceted from the start, Bob Stillman has lit up some of New York's best-known stages as actor, musician, and writer. He has been nominated twice for Tony Awards — first as the actor, composer, and onstage pianist of Dirty Blonde, and later as one of the songwriters for Urban Cowboy.

Bob began studying piano and composition at the Juilliard and Manhattan Schools at the age of nine, and went on to graduate with honors in music from Princeton. That rare double fluency runs through everything he does — on stage, behind the piano, and in the teaching studio.
