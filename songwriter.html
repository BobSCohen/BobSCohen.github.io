The Songwriter

Bob's original songs fold folk-rock, jazz, pop, and classical influences into a sound all his own. His debut album Come Down Angel was co-produced with Tom Corwin (Bonnie Raitt, Stevie Wonder), and his theatrical songwriting reaches from Broadway to children's television.
